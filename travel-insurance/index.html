Travel Insurance in Ontario

A trip should be about the destination, not worry about what happens if plans change or someone gets sick away from home. Travel insurance covers medical emergencies and trip disruptions, so you can travel with confidence.

What is covered

Travel policies can be arranged for a single trip or for frequent travel. Dilbag helps you choose the structure and limits that fit your trip, your health, and your plans.

  • Emergency medical. Coverage for hospital care, doctor visits, and emergency treatment while travelling outside your home province or country.
  • Trip cancellation. Reimbursement for prepaid, non-refundable trip costs if a covered reason forces you to cancel before departure.
  • Trip interruption. Coverage for unused trip costs and extra travel expenses if a covered event cuts your trip short.
  • Baggage and personal effects. Protection for lost, stolen, or damaged luggage and belongings during your trip.
  • Travel delay and missed connection. Help with meals, lodging, and rebooking costs when a covered delay disrupts your plans.
  • Annual and multi-trip plans. Coverage for several trips in a year, useful for frequent travellers and snowbirds who travel often.

What to know

Your provincial health plan covers only part of medical costs outside Ontario, and very little outside Canada. Emergency care abroad can be expensive, which is why medical coverage is often the most important part of a travel policy.

Pre-existing health conditions, trip length, destination, and activities all affect what a policy covers and what it costs. Dilbag asks about those details up front, explains exclusions and stability requirements, and helps you choose limits that make sense for the trip.

As a Registered Insurance Broker with more than 25 years of experience, Dilbag keeps the process straightforward. Share your travel dates and destination, and he will walk through the options before you book or before you leave.
