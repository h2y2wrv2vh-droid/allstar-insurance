What to know

What to know

Trucking rates reflect your CVOR record, driver experience, radius of operation, cargo, and claims history. Two fleets that look similar on paper can price very differently once those details are reviewed. Dilbag works through your fleet list, driver records, and routes so nothing important is missed.

Cargo limits deserve the same care. A limit set too low leaves a full load exposed, while a limit set too high costs more than it needs to. Dilbag reviews what you haul and helps you set a limit that fits a typical load.
