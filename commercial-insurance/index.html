Commercial Insurance in Ontario

Larger operations face larger and more layered risks: multiple locations, bigger fleets, more contracts, and more people depending on the business. Commercial insurance is built to fit that scale, with coverage arranged around your actual exposures.

Coverage

What is covered

Commercial programs are tailored to the operation. Dilbag reviews your locations, activities, vehicles, and contractual obligations, then assembles the coverage your business needs.

Commercial property

Coverage for buildings, equipment, stock, and tenant improvements across one or several locations.

Commercial general liability

Protection against third-party injury and property damage claims connected to your operations, premises, or products.

Commercial auto and fleet

Coverage for business vehicles and fleets, including liability, collision, and comprehensive protection.

Business interruption

Help with ongoing costs and lost income while the operation recovers after a covered loss.

Professional and errors coverage

Protection for firms whose advice, design, or services could lead to a claim of financial loss.

Specialty and additional lines

Coverage for exposures such as cargo, equipment in transit, cyber events, or directors and officers liability, where the operation requires it.

What to know
