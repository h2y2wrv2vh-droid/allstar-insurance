Life and Financial Services in Ontario

Life and financial protection is about the people who depend on you. Dilbag helps families and business owners put a clear plan in place, with coverage that fits their stage of life and their budget.

Coverage

What is covered

Life and financial services cover more than a single policy. Dilbag reviews your situation and explains the options that fit your family, your obligations, and your long term plans.

Term life insurance

Protection for a set period at a level premium, often used to cover a mortgage, income, or family expenses during working years.

Permanent life insurance

Lifelong protection that can also build cash value, used where long term coverage or estate planning is the goal.

Disability protection

Income support if an illness or injury keeps you from working, helping household costs continue during recovery.

Critical illness protection

A lump sum benefit if you are diagnosed with a covered serious illness, to help with treatment costs, time off, or family needs.

Business and key person protection

Coverage that helps a business continue if an owner or key person dies or becomes seriously ill.

Planning conversations

Reviews of beneficiaries, coverage amounts, and gaps, so your plan stays current as life changes.
