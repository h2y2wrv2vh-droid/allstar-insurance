Motorcycle Insurance in Ontario

Riding is different from driving, and the insurance should reflect that. Dilbag helps riders find coverage for the bike, the gear, and the liability that comes with two wheels.

Coverage

What is covered

Motorcycle policies combine mandatory coverage with options that fit how and when you ride. Dilbag explains each part in plain terms so you can choose with confidence.

Third-party liability

Protection if you are found responsible for injury to someone else or damage to their property while riding.

Collision coverage

Coverage to repair or replace your motorcycle after a collision, even when you are at fault.

Comprehensive coverage

Protection against theft, vandalism, fire, and certain weather losses that are not collisions.

Uninsured motorist coverage

Protection if you are hit by a driver with no insurance or by a driver who leaves the scene.

Riding gear and custom parts

Coverage options for helmets, jackets, and custom parts and equipment added to your bike.

Seasonal and lay-up options

Options that reflect Ontario riding seasons, including reduced coverage while the bike is stored in winter.

What to know

What to know

Your premium reflects your licence class, riding experience, the bike, and how you use it. A new rider on a high-powered machine pays very differently from an experienced rider on a commuter bike. Small details, like where the bike is stored and how far you ride, matter too.

Dilbag reviews those details with you, lists your gear and custom parts so they are covered, and compares quotes across the insurers available through Aaxel Insurance Brokers Ltd. The goal is a policy that holds up after a claim, not just the lowest number on the page.
