Renters Insurance in Ontario

Your landlord insures the building, not your belongings. Renters insurance covers what is yours, protects you if you are found responsible for damage, and helps with living costs if a covered loss forces you out.

Coverage

What is covered

A renters policy is simple and affordable. Dilbag helps you size the contents and liability limits to your home, so you are covered without paying for more than you need.

Contents and belongings

Furniture, electronics, clothing, and personal items, covered against fire, theft, water losses, and other insured events.

Tenant liability

Protection if you accidentally damage the rental or someone is injured in your home and you are found responsible.

Additional living expenses

Help with hotel, meal, and temporary housing costs if a covered loss makes your rental unlivable.

Coverage that follows you

Your belongings are covered while you are away from home too, subject to policy limits, such as items stolen from your car.

Low cost protection

Renters coverage is often one of the most affordable policies you will buy, with limits you can adjust to your belongings.

Landlord requirements

Many leases and buildings ask for proof of renters insurance. Dilbag provides the documents your landlord needs.

What to know

What to know

The most common misunderstanding about renting is that the landlord policy covers the tenant. It does not. It covers the building and the landlord's property. If a fire, break-in, or burst pipe damages your belongings, replacing them is on you unless you have your own policy.

Dilbag walks through what you own room by room, so the contents limit reflects real replacement value rather than a guess. He also explains the liability limit, which is the part of the policy that protects you if your actions cause damage to the building or injury to someone else.
