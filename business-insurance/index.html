Business Insurance in Ontario

A small business carries real risk: a customer injury, a damaged storefront, a lost week of work. Business insurance helps you keep going after a setback, with coverage shaped around what your business does every day.

Coverage

What is covered

Business policies are assembled from the pieces your operation needs. Dilbag reviews your work, your premises, and your obligations, then builds a package that fits.

Commercial general liability

Protection if your business is found legally responsible for injury to a third party or damage to their property.

Business property

Coverage for your building, equipment, inventory, and improvements if a covered loss damages them.

Business interruption

Help with ongoing expenses and lost income while your business recovers after a covered loss.

Professional liability

Protection for businesses that give advice or provide professional services, if a client claims an error or omission caused them harm.

Crime coverage

Protection against losses from employee dishonesty, theft, or fraud that affect your business funds or property.

Equipment and tools

Coverage for the equipment your business depends on, including items used at job sites or in transit.
