Home Insurance in Ontario

Your home is where daily life happens, and where much of what you own lives. Home insurance helps you rebuild, replace, and recover after the unexpected, with coverage fitted to the house you actually live in.

What is covered

A home policy is built from several parts working together. Dilbag walks through each part with you, so you know what your policy does before you ever need it.

  • Dwelling protection. Help to repair or rebuild your house after a covered loss such as fire, wind, or vandalism.
  • Personal belongings. Coverage for furniture, electronics, clothing, and other household items, at home and often while temporarily away.
  • Additional living expenses. Support for temporary housing, meals, and related costs if a covered loss makes your home unlivable.
  • Personal liability. Protection if you are found legally responsible for injury to someone else or damage to their property.
  • Detached structures. Coverage for garages, sheds, fences, and other structures on your property that are separate from the house.
  • Optional additions. Extras that may fit your home, such as water damage protection, service line coverage, or higher limits for valuables.
