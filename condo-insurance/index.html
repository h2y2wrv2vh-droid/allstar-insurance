Condo Insurance in Ontario

Your condo corporation insures the building, not everything inside your unit. Condo insurance covers your belongings, your improvements, and the gaps the master policy leaves, so a claim does not land on you alone.

Coverage

What is covered

A condo policy is built around what the master policy does not cover. Dilbag reviews your corporation documents with you so the limits match your unit, not a generic template.

Contents and belongings

Furniture, electronics, clothing, and personal belongings inside your unit, covered against insured losses.

Improvements and betterments

Upgrades you made to your unit, such as flooring, counters, or fixtures, beyond the standard finish the corporation covers.

Loss assessment

Your share of a large building claim when the corporation assesses owners for an amount the master policy does not pay.

Personal liability

Protection if someone is injured in your unit or your actions damage someone else's property.

Additional living expenses

Help with hotel and meal costs if a covered loss makes your unit unlivable while it is repaired.

Water damage options

Coverage options for water losses, including sudden leaks and, where available, sewer backup, explained clearly before you choose.

What to know

What to know

The master policy covers common elements and the standard unit as defined by your corporation. Everything beyond that, from your contents to the upgrades you installed, is yours to protect. Many owners only learn where the line sits after a claim.

Dilbag reviews your corporation certificate, by-laws, and the standard unit definition with you, then compares quotes across the insurers available through Aaxel Insurance Brokers Ltd. The goal is a policy that matches your building and your unit.

As a Registered Insurance Broker with more than 25 years of experience, Dilbag also helps after a loss. He explains how your policy and the corporation policy work together, and what to document for a claim.
